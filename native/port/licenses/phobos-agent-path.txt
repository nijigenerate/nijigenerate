two-segment buildPath behavior. No third-party test or fixture is generated.

The private AgentPanel foreach UTF-8 decoder follows the selected
core.internal.utf.decode(char[]) body used by rt.aApplycd2.
Copyright Digital Mars 2003-2016. Authors: Walter Bright, Sean Kelly.
Source: core/internal/utf.d in the installed independent LDC 1.41 library.
Upstream: https://github.com/dlang/dmd/blob/v2.111.0/druntime/src/core/internal/utf.d
Foreach adapter: https://github.com/dlang/dmd/blob/v2.111.0/druntime/src/rt/aApply.d

The private AgentPanel string-strip reverse byte phase follows std.string.
Copyright The D Language Foundation 2007-.
Authors: Walter Bright, Andrei Alexandrescu, Jonathan M Davis,
and David L. 'SpottedTiger' Davis.
Source: std/string.d in the installed independent LDC 1.41 library.

The private AgentPanel char-string join follows std.array.join's
cheap-iteration, same-encoding path.
